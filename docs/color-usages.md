For the **Savarrah** website, here’s a guide on how to use the brand colors effectively:

### 1. **Primary Color (Blue)**:
   - **Usage:** Blue will dominate the overall design since it’s the primary color.
   - **Where to Use:**
     - **Header and Navigation:** Use blue for the background of the header and navigation bar, ensuring the logo and menu items stand out.
     - **Buttons:** Apply blue for primary action buttons like "Submit," "Login," and "View Details" to make them prominent.
     - **Links and Highlights:** Use blue for important links and call-to-actions throughout the site.
     - **Footer Background:** Maintain brand consistency by using blue in the footer.

### 2. **Secondary Color (Green)**:
   - **Usage:** Green will be used as an accent to complement the blue.
   - **Where to Use:**
     - **Secondary Buttons or Links:** Use green for secondary actions, like "Cancel" or "Back" buttons.
     - **Headings or Highlights:** Add green to highlight important sections such as pricing, form labels, or status updates (e.g., "Completed" or "In Progress").
     - **Icons or Illustrations:** Incorporate green into icons or small decorative elements to break the monotony of blue.

### 3. **Least Used Color (White)**:
   - **Usage:** White will mostly serve as a background or to balance out the design.
   - **Where to Use:**
     - **Backgrounds:** Use white for the main background of pages, ensuring the content (text and images) stands out.
     - **Text on Dark Backgrounds:** When using blue or green for backgrounds (e.g., header, footer), white text will ensure readability.
     - **Forms and Input Fields:** White can be used for the background of form input fields for a clean and modern look.

### Example Layout:
- **Header:** Blue background with white text and green accent for hover states or active navigation.
- **Buttons:** Primary buttons in blue with white text, secondary buttons in green.
- **Order Status Tags:** "Completed" in green, "Pending" in blue for consistency.

This balance will keep the design clean and professional while highlighting important elements with blue and green.
