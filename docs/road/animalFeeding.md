Designing the **Savarrah Animal Feeding Page** requires careful consideration of layout, content, and user experience. Here’s a detailed breakdown of how the page should look and function:

### Layout Overview

1. **Header**

   - **Logo**: Positioned at the top left, linking back to the homepage.
   - **Navigation Menu**: Links to other pages (Printing, Animal Feeding, Fresh Oil, Godown, Stationery, Hardware, etc.).
   - **Call to Action (CTA)**: A prominent button for users to "Order Now" or "Get a Quote."

2. **Hero Section**

   - **Background Image**: A visually appealing image related to animal feeding (e.g., livestock, animal feed).
   - **Headline**: Bold and engaging, e.g., “Quality Animal Feed for Every Need.”
   - **Subheading**: A brief description, e.g., “Providing the best nutrition for your livestock.”
   - **CTA Button**: “Explore Our Products” leading to the product listings.

3. **Service Listings**

   - **Grid Layout**: Showcasing different types of animal feed services.
   - **Cards for Each Service**:
     - **Image**: Relevant image representing each feed type (e.g., grain, pellets).
     - **Service Name**: e.g., “Cattle Feed,” “Poultry Feed,” etc.
     - **Short Description**: A brief overview of the service.
     - **CTA Button**: “Learn More” button that navigates to detailed service descriptions.

4. **Pricing Information**

   - **Table or Grid Layout**: Clear pricing for different types of animal feeds.
   - **Columns**:
     - **Feed Type**: Name of the feed.
     - **Price**: Cost per unit (e.g., per bag).
     - **Quantity Options**: Dropdowns or input fields for selecting quantities.
     - **Order Button**: For quick access to the order form.

5. **Order Form**

   - **Form Fields**:
     - **User Information**: Name, contact number, email.
     - **Feed Selection**: Dropdown or checklist for selecting feed types.
     - **Quantity**: Input for specifying the amount.
     - **Delivery Options**: Selection for delivery or pick-up.
     - **Special Instructions**: Optional field for additional requests.
   - **Submit Button**: “Place Order” button to submit the form.

6. **Testimonials Section**

   - **Customer Feedback**: Quotes from satisfied customers about the quality of the animal feed and services.
   - **Star Ratings**: Visual representation of ratings (e.g., 4/5 stars).

7. **Contact Section**

   - **Contact Information**: Phone number, email address, and physical address.
   - **Contact Form**: For inquiries about services or feedback.
   - **Social Media Links**: Icons linking to relevant social media profiles.

8. **Footer**
   - **Links**: Quick links to other pages (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service).
   - **Copyright Notice**: Year and company name.

### Visual Design Elements

- **Color Scheme**: Use a cohesive color palette that aligns with the brand (blue, green, and white). For example:

  - **Background**: Light colors for readability.
  - **Text**: Darker colors for contrast.
  - **Buttons**: Use brand colors for CTA buttons to make them stand out.

- **Typography**:
  - **Headings**: Bold, large font for headings to draw attention.
  - **Body Text**: Clean, readable font for descriptions and details.
- **Spacing**: Ample white space between sections to prevent clutter and enhance readability.

- **Responsive Design**: Ensure the layout adapts well on mobile and tablet devices. Use media queries for layout adjustments.

### User Experience Considerations

- **Intuitive Navigation**: Make it easy for users to find information and place orders.
- **Loading Speed**: Optimize images and scripts for fast loading times.
- **Accessibility**: Ensure text contrast is high for readability and that the site is navigable via keyboard for users with disabilities.
- **Feedback Mechanisms**: Provide users with feedback after form submissions (e.g., confirmation messages).
